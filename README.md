# Jade Room Digital Experience

Act as a world-class luxury hospitality brand designer, UX/UI designer, conversion strategist, local SEO expert, and senior front-end developer.

Design and build a premium, bilingual website for Jade Room, a modern Chinese restaurant in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

BRAND OVERVIEW

Brand name: Jade Room

Arabic name: جايد روم

Location: Sama Gallery, Al Malqa, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Concept: Modern Chinese dining

Positioning: Sophisticated and contemporary, with a warm and inviting atmosphere

Experience: Elevated Chinese food designed for sharing, refined hospitality, beautiful presentation, and a memorable social dining experience

Primary audience:

- Riyadh residents looking for premium Chinese dining

- Couples and small groups

- Families and social gatherings

- Business lunches and dinners

- Visitors searching for the best Chinese restaurants in Riyadh

The website must communicate sophistication and quality without appearing overly formal, traditional, dark, or intimidating.

PRIMARY WEBSITE GOALS

1. Introduce Jade Room and establish its positioning as a leading modern Chinese restaurant in Riyadh.

2. Encourage visitors to reserve a table.

3. Rank organically for high-intent restaurant searches in English and Arabic.

4. Make it easy to view the menu, location, opening hours, contact information, and restaurant atmosphere.

5. Create a fast, mobile-first experience because most visitors will arrive from Google, Instagram, TikTok, and map searches.

PRIMARY CTA

Use “Reserve a Table” as the main English CTA.

Use “احجز طاولتك” as the main Arabic CTA.

Every reservation button should open Jade Room’s external SevenRooms reservation page in a new tab.

Use this placeholder until the final link is added:

[SEVENROOMS_RESERVATION_URL]

Do not build an internal reservation form.

LANGUAGES

Build a complete bilingual experience:

- English

- Arabic with proper RTL layout

Add a clear EN | عربي language switcher in the header.

Arabic pages must be culturally natural and professionally written, not literal machine translations. The Arabic layout must switch completely to RTL, including navigation, text alignment, icons, spacing, and content direction.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create an elegant, editorial, contemporary Chinese identity.

The visual direction should feel:

- Refined

- Atmospheric

- Modern

- Warm

- Confident

- Distinctive

- Premium but approachable

Avoid:

- Generic restaurant templates

- Obvious Chinese clichés

- Red-and-gold overload

- Dragons, lantern illustrations, bamboo patterns, or pagodas

- Excessive animations

- Heavy black backgrounds across the entire website

- Corporate hotel-restaurant styling

- Neon or nightclub aesthetics

- Large blocks of unnecessary text

Suggested palette:

- Deep jade green as the signature color

- Warm ivory or rice-paper cream

- Charcoal or soft black

- Muted brass accents

- Optional restrained oxblood accent

Typography:

- Use an elegant editorial serif for headlines

- Use a highly legible contemporary sans-serif for supporting text

- Select an Arabic typeface that feels equally refined and works naturally with the English typography

- Maintain excellent readability on mobile

Use generous spacing, strong photography, restrained animation, subtle texture, and carefully composed layouts. The design should feel inspired by a premium modern Chinese dining room, not by a takeaway restaurant.
